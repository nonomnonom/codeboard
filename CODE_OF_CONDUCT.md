# Code of Conduct

Treat people with respect. Critique the work with evidence, make room for questions, and respect different experience levels and backgrounds.

Harassment, discriminatory language, threats, sexualized conduct, doxxing, and deliberate disruption are not welcome. Do not publish another person's private information or use project spaces for spam.

These expectations apply to issues, pull requests, discussions, reviews, and other spaces maintained for Codeboard. Maintainers may edit or remove content, lock conversations, or restrict participation. They should explain moderation decisions where doing so does not expose private information, and apply the rules consistently.

For a conduct concern, use a private contact listed on [the maintainer's profile](https://github.com/nonomnonom), if available. GitHub's **Report abuse** action is another private route for violations of its platform rules. Do not put sensitive reports in public issues. Maintainers must not disclose a reporter's identity without consent unless required by law.

Disagreement with a technical decision is welcome; personal attacks are not. A moderation appeal can be sent through the same private contact with the decision and relevant context. No response-time guarantee is made.
